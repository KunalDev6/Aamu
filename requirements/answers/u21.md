## Original goal request fails after manual approval

Question: If the original goal request times out or fails after manual steps were approved locally, should those approved steps then sync normally, or stay phone-only until a later successful goal check? This is separate from needs_support, which always makes them localOnly.

## Evening reminder

Question: When should the evening step-completion reminder appear? Please specify its local time, whether tapping it offers Done / Not today, and what should happen if the step is first offered after that time.

Answer: At 18:00 local time, with “Yes” and “Not today” as the two answers. If the step was first offered late in the day, no reminder is sent.
