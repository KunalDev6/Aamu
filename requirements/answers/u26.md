# U26 — Home and Settings redesign

Source: direct user request, 14 November 2026 (Europe/Helsinki).

UI redesign (user feedback: the current Home and Settings feel confusing and not delightful). Keep the existing tokens and font, and change the layout:

1. **Full-bleed sky:** the landscape is the full-screen background on Home and the morning flow, not a boxed card. The sky follows local time (night/dawn/day/dusk). The sun is unlit (outline) unless a hold is confirmed today; with a partner, each half lights separately.
2. **Home shows exactly one state card at the bottom, and nothing else:**
   - night/before the alarm window: large light-weight next alarm time (around 56 pt, weight 300) centred on the sky, with “Alarm tomorrow” above and “Sleep well” below;
   - in the 180-minute early window: “I’m already up”;
   - after the hold: “You’re up.” plus a partner line if paired, and a card with “Today’s step · {size}”, the step text, a Done primary button and “Too much today” as quiet text;
   - from 18:00 until midnight while the step is unanswered: “Did you get to it?” with the step text, a Yes primary button and Not today;
   - alarm off: “Alarm is off today.” with a Turn alarm on primary button.
3. **One warm-accent primary button per screen.** Everything else is outlined or text.
4. **Header:** small “aamu” on the left; small “Help” and “Settings” text buttons on the right, always visible on Home with at least 4.5:1 contrast. Remove the bottom Help pill.
5. **Settings:** “‹ Back” in the top-left corner. Grouped list rows with current values on the right, no stacked pills. Group 1: Alarm (07:00 · On, with time and on/off inside), Goals (count), Moments (count), Place sound (Sea/Forest/Mine). Group 2: Partner (Paired/Not paired), Your people (Clinic on/off). Remove the duplicate “Your alarm” card. Rename “Voice messages” to “Moments” once that change lands.
6. Use a realistic alarm time (07:00) for screenshots.
7. Send screenshots of all five Home states plus Settings, at 07:30 and 21:00.

## U26 clarification — confirmed user selections

The user confirmed the two follow-up choices for this redesign:

- Keep adaptive dawn while ringing.
- Keep Moments and the past-self note as quiet links inside the one Home card.

These clarify U26; they do not remove the earlier adaptive timing rules or introduce another Home card.
