# U30 — moments replace own-voice morning messages

Source: direct user messages, 14 November 2026 (Europe/Helsinki), in the Claude Code session. The changes were made in that session, not by the Codex agent.

## Background the user reported

The user asked the challenge hosts (Beyond the Clinic mentors) four questions on 14 November and reported their answers:

- Smart sensing: “whatever you can track at home however you want”.
- Hearing your own good-day voice: “physically hearing your voice recorded in general is bad”.
- Sleep: “focus on one sleep metric?”
- Worrying goals: “this is open part of the challenge but defenitly want the clinic to see the data s they are proffesional”.

The user then rejected written messages (“words mean nothing in my opinion like seeing a motivitonal quote is kinda bad”), asked “but they would need to save a good pic they like?”, and after the answer told the session to “Build moments”.

## Decision

A morning message becomes a **moment**: one photo the person chooses, plus an optional recording (up to 20 seconds) of the sound around it. No text and no spoken message. This supersedes the earlier decisions that the morning message is the person's own recorded voice. Selection order, gentler-only fallback, Rest, restore and learning counts are unchanged.

## What was built

- `mobile/src/moments.ts`: opens the system photo picker for one image and copies it into the phone's private, backup-excluded folder (the same folder as recordings). The picker is loaded only when used. Only the app's own copies are displayed. Removing a moment deletes its files.
- `mobile/src/MomentEditor.tsx`, `MomentPhoto.tsx`: choose a photo, optionally “Add the sound of this place” (existing recorder, tap to start, 20 seconds), “Save moment”. Copy: “Pick a photo from a day you liked. It doesn’t need to be a good photo.”
- Setup step 2 / Settings → Moments: per-level cards with thumbnails, “Add a moment”, “Remove”, and “Resting moments” with restore.
- Morning message screen (`app/message.tsx`) and demo morning: the photo appears in a rounded frame, fades in over two seconds and drifts slowly (still under Reduce Motion). The heading is “A moment you saved.” Controls are “Play the sound” (only when there is one) and “Rest this moment”. A photo-only moment offers the step when shown and counts as the day's one play after three seconds on screen.
- Home: “See your moment”; on an easy morning after the hold, “Save a moment from today” opens the editor for the easier-morning level.
- `MessageClip` gains optional `photoUri`; a photo-only moment has an empty `uri` and zero duration. Existing recordings without a photo keep working with their old labels.
- New dependency `expo-image-picker ~58.0.10` with its config plugin (photo-library text; camera blocked; microphone text kept identical to expo-audio's so Android recording is not blocked). Recorded in NOTES.md.
- The iOS project was regenerated with `expo prebuild --platform ios --no-install`, which recreated `mobile/ios/` from configuration, and pods were reinstalled (ExpoImagePicker linked). Any signing team chosen by hand in Xcode would need choosing again; none was present in the regenerated project or `app.json`.

## Not done

- The five-second voice sensing sample, and reply notes (“Say something back”, “A note from your past self”), are unchanged. Whether to drop them after the hosts' feedback is still the user's decision.
- “One sleep metric” wording in the clinic view and pitch.
- No moments are seeded; presenters add their own.

## Evidence

- 364 API/shared tests, API and mobile typechecks and mobile lint pass.
- Browser preview at 390 × 844: the Moments step listed a test photo with Remove; a sensed demo morning showed “A moment you saved.” with the photo, “Rest this moment” and the step. The test photo was removed from the preview afterwards.
- Not verified: picking a real photo on an iPhone or Android phone, recording and playing the place sound with a photo, the real (non-demo) morning message screen, and backup exclusion of a copied photo on a device.
