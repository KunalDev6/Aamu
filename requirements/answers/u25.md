# U25 — Home presentation and copy corrections

User instructions received 14 November 2026. Verbatim text below; alternatives are retained exactly as supplied.

1. The greeting follows local time (morning before 12:00, afternoon 12:00–17:59, evening from 18:00; put these in config/copy), or remove it.
2. The sky must follow the current local time; 19:33 should render dusk/night, not dawn.
3. The sun shows a filled state only after a confirmed hold (or partner_up for the partner half). With alarm off or no hold today, render the unlit outline.
4. “That’s enough for now.” is wrong. The correct copy is “That’s enough for today.”, shown only after removing the step at Tiny, with a 5-second Undo, then dismissed. Never show it when no step was offered or on off days, and don’t make it the largest text on Home.
5. Move “Preview scenes” behind the demo-mode flag in the hidden demo controls.
6. Give the header arrow icon a word label, or remove it. No unlabelled icon buttons.
7. Make “Turn alarm on” the single warm-accent primary button.
8. Make Help clearly visible: text contrast of at least 4.5:1 and a visible outline. Check the contrast of Settings and secondary buttons too.
9. Round the landscape card to the same large radius as the other cards (no hard top edge), or make it full-bleed.
10. Show the next alarm time on Home (when off: “Alarm is off today.” plus, once turned on, “Next alarm: tomorrow {time}”).
    Report each fix with a screenshot at 07:00 and at 19:30.
