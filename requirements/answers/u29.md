# U29 — sun always visible, partner clarity, and a demo morning that senses itself

Source: direct user messages, 14 November 2026 (Europe/Helsinki), in the Claude Code session. The user also said: “we're in the repo of the app you can look through it and tell me what it needs or even change it”. The code changes below were made in that session, not by the Codex agent.

## User decisions (verbatim where quoted)

- “i just want the sun to show at all times and it should be ovbious its their partner thats up”. The sun outline stays visible in every state, including night. When paired, it must be obvious which half is the partner.
- The partner is another Aamu user paired by code. A web page for a friend or family member who does not use the app was discussed as a stretch and is **not built**.

## Changes made (14 November 2026, about 22:30–22:45 local)

1. **Demo morning senses itself.** `shared/demo/session.ts`: new default choice `sensed`. The level comes from that demo morning's own snooze count and getting-up time through the existing `scoreMorning` rules; no voice measurement is invented (the voice term is left out). Easy / Medium / Hard remain as labelled overrides. This extends U24's “selected morning difficulty”; it does not remove it.
2. **Fast-time snooze.** In fast time a demo snooze re-rings by itself after 8 seconds and counts as the 5 minutes it stands for; ringing time counts twelvefold (10 s ≈ 2 min). Normal time keeps the five-minute snooze. This supersedes the earlier demo note “Snooze stays five minutes” for fast time only.
3. **Sensed mornings skip the simulated five-second voice card.** After the hold the screen shows “You’re up.” while the sunrise finishes (3 s), then the step. Picked levels keep the five-second stage.
4. **Demo screen copy.** The caption shows what was sensed, for example “Sensed · Hard morning · 2 snoozes · 14 min to get up”. “No need to reply.” appears only when a message is playing.
5. **Home.** A missing alarm permission is now the primary action (“The 7:00 alarm can’t ring on this phone yet.” + “Allow alarms”), and “Sleep well” is hidden while it is pending. Before the hold, one line says “The sun fills when you get up.” or “Your partner is up.” In demo mode Home has “Try a morning · Demo”, which opens a fast, sensed demo ring (`/demo?start=1`).
6. **Sun.** Paired suns have a thin gap between the halves, a divider when neither is lit, and “You” / “Partner” tags on opaque surfaces above the sun (Home, alarm and demo morning).
7. **Sunrise visibility.** The alarm and demo morning screens use the same clear-horizon scrim as Home, so the dawn is no longer under a 72% dark overlay.
8. **Hold button** label changed from “Hold to begin” to “Hold: I’m up” (alarm, demo, design review).

## Evidence
