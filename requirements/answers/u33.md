## What was built

1. **Start demo (Home, demo mode only).** Replaces “Try a morning”. The guided flow is:
   goal editor for a new goal → on Approve, an easier morning rings → after the step appears, “Next morning” → a hard morning (the presenter snoozes twice, then holds) → “Next” → Help, where “Ask my clinic to call me” is the last action. `mobile/src/demo/tour.ts` keeps the stage in memory only.
   - The guided mornings use a fresh demo history, fast time and sensed levels, offer only the goal just approved (`onlyGoalId`), and start from a carried size of Full (`initialCarry`) so the easier morning shows the Full step and the hard morning shows Tiny.
   - The simulated partner gets up 2.5 seconds after the person's hold. This is a simulation, as in U24.
   - The ringing screen shows the alarm time plus five minutes per snooze instead of the real clock. Demo controls, “Home” and “Partner gets up now” are hidden during the guided flow; “Close” leaves it.
   - If three approved goals already exist, the goal stage is skipped and the most recent approved goal is used.
2. **Thompson sampling (U16's conditional stretch, now built).** `selectMessage` takes an optional random source. With it, the final scoring step draws from each moment's Beta(finished-step count + 1, other plays + 1) posterior and picks the highest draw. Filtering by level, gentler-only fallback, skipping yesterday's moment, unplayed-first and tie order are unchanged. Without a random source the fixed score is used, so existing callers and tests are unaffected. The real morning screen and the demo morning pass `Math.random`; tests use a seeded generator.
3. **“How the AI checked these steps”** in the goal editor: shown only when the server sends its demo-mode details (`DEMO_MODE=true`) and suggested steps are being used. It states that Gemma wrote the steps and Gemini Decision judged the goal and estimated each step's duration, and shows the most likely duration with its probability and a small bar per step. No distress probability is shown.

- Browser preview at 390 × 844, with hand-written steps because the browser cannot call the API: Start demo → goal “Get outside more” approved → easier morning “Sensed · Easy morning · 0 snoozes · 3 min to get up”, Full step “Walk outside for 30 minutes”, both sun halves lit → Next morning → two snoozes → “Sensed · Hard morning · 2 snoozes · 14 min to get up”, Tiny step “Stand outside for 1 minute” → Next → Help with “Your clinic” and “Ask my clinic to call me”. No “Demo” wording appeared on these screens.

## Follow-up, same evening (about 23:30 local)

User feedback (verbatim): “when the animation plays it loads slowly and at the top it just goes straight to the top and then it just turns both circles yellow it should start as the partners side already filled and make it ovbious the partner is already up (this is all supposed to be part of the demo showcase as we will just be recording a video)”.

Changes to the guided demo only:

- The simulated partner is **already up when each guided morning rings**. The earlier “2.5 seconds after the person's hold” rule is superseded. The sun starts in place with the Partner half lit and the You half empty.
- The ringing and snoozed screens say “Your partner is already up.” After the hold the screen says “You’re up.” and “You’re both up.”
- The person's half now **fills with the hold itself** over its two seconds and empties again if the hold is released early; only a completed hold keeps it. This replaces the three-second fade that started after the hold. A partial fill is progress feedback, not a confirmed state.

Evidence: browser preview at 390 × 844 showed the ringing screen at 7:00 with “Your partner is already up.” and only the Partner half lit; about 1.1 seconds into a hold the You half was partly filled; releasing early left the alarm ringing and unconfirmed; a completed hold showed “You’re both up.” Mobile typecheck and lint pass. Not verified on the simulator or a phone, and animation smoothness there was not measured.

## Goal ideas (about 23:40 local)

User request (verbatim): “also inside your goal add a button below find my steps that says something along the lines with strugglineg with a goal press here if you need help choosing and it comes with some preset goals”.

Built in `mobile/src/GoalEditor.tsx`: under “Find my steps”, a quiet “Not sure what to choose? See some ideas”. It opens “Some places to start” (“Pick one that feels a little bit true. You can change it afterwards.”) with six goals: Get outside more; Call my friends more often; Cook real meals again; Move my body a little each day; Read more; Get back to a hobby I used to love. Choosing one fills the goal and starts “Find my steps” at once. All six pass the shared safety phrase check.

Evidence: browser preview showed the link, the list, and that “Read more” filled the goal and started a request (which failed in the browser as expected, because the browser preview cannot call the API). Typecheck and lint pass. Not seen on the simulator or a phone with real suggestions.
