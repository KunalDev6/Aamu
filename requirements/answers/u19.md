## No response and stopped

no_response = within 60 minutes of the first ring there was no interaction of any kind: no system Stop, no system snooze, no in-app snooze, Aamu not opened, no hold. stopped = at least one of those interactions occurred but no hold completed within the 60 minutes. Any single snooze or app open is sufficient for stopped. The window is measured from the first ring only; re-rings after snooze do not extend it. Decide the level at the 60-minute mark when the app is running; otherwise decide it on next launch from persisted evidence (ring time, snooze and stop events, app-open timestamps) and backfill every past alarm-enabled day that has no record, then sync. If the app cannot observe whether the system alarm was stopped, record no_response and note the limitation in NOTES.md. Neither level converts to the other afterwards. A late open on a no_response day (after 60 minutes, before local midnight) uses the same late flow as stopped: hold, tiny step, message behind a play button; it sets upAt, publishes partner_up, counts as an offered day for carrySize, and leaves level as no_response. In fast time the 60 minutes are 60 seconds. Tests: untouched alarm → no_response; one system snooze then nothing → stopped; app opened and closed → stopped; backfill of three missed days on next launch; late hold on a no_response day.
