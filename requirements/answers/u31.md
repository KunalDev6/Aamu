# U31 — hidden Demo wording and the goal breakdown ladder

Source: direct user messages, 14 November 2026 (Europe/Helsinki), in the Claude Code session. The changes were made in that session, not by the Codex agent.

## User instructions (verbatim)

- “what about when the user puts their goal and how the user sees it goes how it gets broken down also i dont want them to see demo when tis on demo”
- “how do the steps work is that day by day or all for each day”

## Decisions

1. **Demo wording is hidden from the audience while demo mode stays on.** This supersedes U28's “Show a small, unobtrusive ‘Demo’ label”. The fixtures and simulations are unchanged; only the wording is hidden. The Demo control panel still says what it is.
2. **The goal screen must show how a goal is broken down** and that each morning offers one step in one size.

## What was built

- `mobile/src/demo/config.ts`: `demoLabelsVisible` (true only when `EXPO_PUBLIC_DEMO_LABELS=true`) and `demoText(labelled, plain)`. Hidden by default.
- Hidden wording: the Home “Demo” tag; “Try a morning · Demo” → “Try a morning”; the demo morning header (“aamu”, “Close”, “Options”, no “Demo morning N” caption; the “Sensed · …” line stays); Settings link “Demo” → “More”; Help and Your people clinic wording (“Your clinic”, “Clinic sharing”, “Clinic code”, “Asked for a call”); the safe person's “(demo)” suffix and “Demo: would call …” button text.
- Clinic web page: opening it with `?present=1` hides the Demo badge, the fictional-profiles notice and the footer. Data and behaviour are unchanged.
- `mobile/src/StepLadder.tsx`: one ladder with a rail and growing dots. Each rung shows the size, its time limit (“At home” for Tiny) and the morning it is for: “For a hard morning”, “For an in-between morning”, “For an easier morning”.
- Goal editor: an introduction (“Aamu turns your goal into one step in three sizes. Each morning you get just one of them: the size that fits how that morning went.”); a pulsing placeholder ladder while steps are being written (still under Reduce Motion); suggestions and editable steps shown on the ladder in one card with two-line fields; “You can change any step. Finishing a step lets the next one grow a size.”; once steps are on screen the request button becomes the quiet “Suggest steps again”, so Approve is the only primary action.
- Goals list: each approved goal shows its three steps on the ladder, and the heading explains that one step is offered per morning.

## Honesty note

With the wording hidden, the simulated clinic call request, the simulated safe-person call and the simulated partner match look real on screen. The Help card no longer says that no real clinician will call. The presenters need to say this in the video or to the judges. Demo builds must not be given to real patients in this state.

## Evidence

- 364 API/shared tests and 35 clinic tests pass; API and mobile typechecks and mobile lint pass.
- iOS simulator (new build with expo-image-picker, served by Metro): the goal editor showed a user-entered goal with generated Tiny/Small/Full steps on the new ladder, fitting one screen.
- Not verified: the hidden-label screens after the change (Home, Help, Your people, demo morning), the clinic page's `?present=1` view in a browser, and anything on a physical phone.
