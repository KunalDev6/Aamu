Verbatim answer text captured for reconciliation. These answers supersede earlier conflicting requirements. Questions are summarized as labels only; do not treat a label as extra user instruction.

## Voice scoring

Confirmed. A valid silent recording uses the same gate as skipped: +1 only when the rolling window has n >= 5 eligible mornings and the person usually speaks; otherwise 0, including when n < 5. Remove the unconditional point for speechOnsetMs = null. For spoke recordings the delayed-onset and quiet-speech rules continue unchanged against the fixed baselines, and apply only once the relevant baseline has at least 5 valid recordings. The voice term is capped at 1 point in total, whichever rules fire. Add tests for: silent with n = 4 (0), silent with a usual speaker (1), silent with a usual skipper (0), and a spoke recording that triggers both delayed-onset and quiet-speech (1, not 2).

## Re-enabling an off alarm

Turning the alarm back on during an off day. If the current time is before today’s scheduled alarm time: reschedule today’s alarm at its original time (system and in-app), clear the off status so today is a normal pending day, and show the “I’m already up” control if within its window. No minimum lead time. If the scheduled time has already passed: today remains off with all off-day rules unchanged; schedule the next alarm for tomorrow; do not ring, do not start any hold flow, and do not create or modify a morning record beyond level: "off". Show one transient line: “Your alarm is back on for tomorrow at {time}.” Tests: re-enable before the time restores today’s alarm and status; re-enable one minute before; re-enable after the time keeps today off and schedules tomorrow; no ring and no record changes in the after case.

## Insufficient clinic observations

Add clinic status insufficient with the label “Not enough recent mornings”. Apply it when the last 7 days contain fewer than 3 observed mornings (any level other than off; days with no record at all are not observed). Never output steady in that case, and never carry a previous status forward as the current one. Persist each user’s last classified status and its date; include them in the reason as a fact. Reason templates: “Alarm off for N days. Last status: X, D days ago.”; “No mornings recorded in the last 7 days. Last status: X, D days ago.”; “Started D days ago. N mornings recorded.” (no previous status). Sort order: might_need_call, drifting, insufficient, steady; within insufficient, longest time since the last observed morning first. If the last 7 days have at least 3 observed mornings but the preceding 14 days have fewer than 5, skip the rules that compare against the earlier average and apply only the count-based rules; if none fire, the status is steady, with “limited history” appended to the reason. Thresholds (3 and 5) in config. Tests: brand-new user, all-off week, all-off 21 days, 2 observed mornings, 3 observed mornings with a thin comparison window, and previous status surfaced in the reason.

## Scheduling and timezones

Scheduling for this build: one daily alarm time plus a single on/off switch. No weekday selection, no multiple alarms, no one-off skip; list weekday selection under “not built” in NOTES.md. Off remains off until switched back on, with the agreed off-day rules. Time handling: schedule the alarm in the phone’s current timezone as wall-clock time, so it follows the phone when travelling; display times in the phone’s 12/24-hour setting and locale. localDate for a morning is the phone’s local date at the first ring (or at the early hold), fixed on the record from then on; store the UTC offset in minutes alongside it. All end-of-day rules (late-flow cutoff, stepDone becoming null, carrySize update, sun reset) use local midnight in the timezone recorded for that morning. Reschedule the next alarm when the system reports a timezone or clock change. One record per localDate; do not handle duplicated or skipped dates from travel, and note this limit in NOTES.md. Add utcOffsetMinutes to the API as optional. Tests: alarm rescheduled after a timezone change, localDate unchanged by a midday timezone change, and midnight rules using the stored offset.

## In-app dawn

In-app dawn (Aamu ringing in the foreground, including “Ring now”): keep 2 / 4 / 6 minutes; in fast time 10 / 15 / 20 seconds. The 20-second landing dawn still applies when Aamu is opened after a system alarm. Classification uses the last three observed mornings within the past 14 days, skipping off: easy = light; medium = middle; hard, stopped, no_response = heavy. Two or more heavy → 6 minutes with a lower starting volume; two or more light → 2 minutes; otherwise 4 minutes. Fewer than three observed mornings in the past 14 days → 4 minutes. A hold completed before the dawn ends takes effect immediately: advance to the voice stage without waiting; the sky continues to full morning with the remaining animation compressed into about 3 seconds (no jump, no freeze); the sun fills and rises on the hold; the place sound fades out over about 2 seconds before any message plays. On snooze, the sky holds its current position and dims slightly; the next ring resumes from there. Durations and thresholds in config. Tests: each classification row, off days skipped, insufficient history, a 15-day gap, and a hold at 10% of the dawn.

## Manual override and localOnly goals

A goal whose latest verdict is needs_support, whether from the local phrase check or from the endpoint, and which the person keeps via “Write my own steps”, is stored with localOnly: true: its title and all three step texts live on the phone only. Exclude localOnly goals from PUT /goals entirely; the server never receives the title, the steps or a marker that such a goal exists. Do not require or suggest changing the title, and do not offer a consent option to send it. Morning records for a localOnly goal send a locally generated opaque goalId, stepSize and stepDone as usual, with stepText: null; make stepText nullable in the API and make sure nothing server-side depends on it or on the goal existing. Rotation, carrySize, per-goal local counts and all Home and message-stage behaviour treat localOnly goals exactly like others. If the person later rewords the goal so that both checks pass, clear localOnly and include it in the next PUT /goals. Tests: localOnly goal absent from PUT /goals, morning record sent with null stepText, rotation across mixed goals, and localOnly cleared after a clean recheck.
