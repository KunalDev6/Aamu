# U35 — sun rises with the hold, size indicator, Start demo restart, separate alarm button

Source: direct user message, 15 November 2026 (Europe/Helsinki), in the Claude Code session. The changes were made in that session, not by the Codex agent.

## User feedback (verbatim)

“the sun doesnt rise when i wake up and the tiny small full buttons dont work or dont makes sense and when i ccik start demo it should go back to beginning where i pick the goal too and a seperate button for make alarm go off”

## Changes

1. **The sun rises with the hold.** In `mobile/src/Landscape.tsx` the sun now rests on the horizon, half behind the hills, until the person is up, whether or not a partner's half is lit. It climbs to a higher position as the person's own half fills (`sunRest` 26, `sunHigh` −30 canvas units). This applies to every screen that draws the sun, including the real Home: after a confirmed hold the sun sits higher than before. The light wave is centred on the risen position.
2. **Size indicator, not buttons.** The Tiny / Small / Full pills on the demo step card looked tappable and were not. They are replaced by three growing dots (as on the goal screen) with today's filled, and a sentence such as “So today's step is Tiny, the smallest of three.”
3. **Start demo always restarts at picking a goal.** It clears unfinished goals and the goals earlier demo runs made (tracked in `demoTourGoalIds`), frees the newest slot if all three are still taken, and resets the demo history. Goals the presenter made outside the demo are kept unless all three slots are full.
4. **Separate “Ring alarm” button on Home (demo mode).** Approving the goal now returns to Home instead of ringing at once. “Ring alarm” rings the next guided morning; outside the guided flow it rings an ordinary fast demo morning. After the easier morning, “Next morning” returns to Home, and “Ring alarm” rings the hard morning. Both buttons show in every Home state.

## Evidence

- 366 API/shared tests, API and mobile typechecks and mobile lint pass.
- Browser preview at 390 × 844 with the real models: Home showed the sun resting on the horizon and the two buttons; Start demo opened an empty goal; a goal from the ideas list was approved and returned to Home; Ring alarm showed 7:00, “Your partner is already up.” and the sun low with the Partner half lit; about 1.1 seconds into the hold the sun had climbed and the You half was filling; after the hold the sun was high with both halves lit; the step card showed “An easier morning: up in 1 min, no snoozes.”, dots with the third filled and “So today's step is Full, the biggest of three.” Next morning returned to Home; Ring alarm, two snoozes and a hold gave “A hard morning: 2 snoozes, 15 min to get up.” and “So today's step is Tiny, the smallest of three.”
- Not verified: the simulator or a phone; text contrast and layout of the lower sun on small screens.
