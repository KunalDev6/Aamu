# U32 — faster step suggestions and steps that are real amounts of the goal

Source: direct user message, 14 November 2026 (Europe/Helsinki), in the Claude Code session. The changes were made in that session, not by the Codex agent.

## User feedback (verbatim)

“the ai seems to very slow and also for example i said goto gym more and the step is literally put on gym shoes? thats not gonna help the person is it if thats all they do”

## Decision

- Each size must be a smaller amount of the goal itself, so that a morning offering only Tiny still does some of the goal. Preparation steps (clothes, shoes, packing, opening an app, planning) are not acceptable suggestions.
- The wait must be short.

## What was changed (`api/src/goals/gemma.ts`)

1. **Thinking set to minimal.** `reasoning_effort: 'minimal'` on the compatible transport and `thinkingConfig.thinkingLevel: 'minimal'` on the native one. Same model (`gemma-4-31b-it`), same single attempt, same 25-second server deadline, no retry. `thinkingBudget: 0` is rejected by this model (“Thinking budget is not supported for this model.”).
2. **New writer instructions.** One activity in three sizes; every size is a real smaller amount of the goal; no getting-ready steps; Tiny is at most two minutes at home right after getting up, using the closest home version when the goal needs another place or other people; a number or duration where natural; one prose example (reading). The JSON shape, the 60-character limit, the untrusted-data line and the no-medical-advice line are unchanged. The verdict fallback variant uses the same instructions.

The running local API was restarted to load the change; `/health` returned 200 locally and through the public tunnel.

## Evidence (synthetic goals only, 14 November 2026, about 23:00 local)

- Same old prompt, “Go to the gym more”: default thinking 20,053 ms and 853 total tokens (27 answer tokens); minimal thinking 1,819 ms and 188 total tokens.
- New prompt, minimal thinking, “Go to the gym more”: 2,145 ms → “Do 10 jumping jacks” / “Do 10 minutes of bodyweight exercises” / “Work out at the gym for 30 minutes”.
- Six further goals, 1.7–2.3 seconds each: Get outside more → Stand outside for 1 minute / Walk outside for 10 minutes / Walk outside for 30 minutes. Call my friends more often → Send one text to a friend / Call a friend for 10 minutes / Call a friend for 30 minutes. Cook real meals again → Chop one vegetable / Cook one side dish / Cook a complete meal. Get back to drawing → Scribble for 1 minute / Sketch for 10 minutes / Draw for 30 minutes. Keep my flat tidy → Put away 3 items / Tidy for 10 minutes / Tidy for 30 minutes. Sleep better → Stretch for 1 minute / 10 minutes / 30 minutes.
- Whole pipeline (`npm run models:probe-pipeline`, goal judge → writer → step judge), three runs: 2,610 ms, 2,673 ms, 2,858 ms, all with a judged ladder. The earlier single observation was 15,574 ms.
- 364 API/shared tests and the API typecheck pass.

These are a handful of observations, not a latency guarantee or a quality evaluation. A vague goal such as “Sleep better” still produces a weak ladder (stretching). The new suggestions were not yet seen in the phone app after the restart.
