## Research-based step writing

- `api/src/goals/evidence.ts` defines four kinds of goal (movement, outdoors, social, other). Each has writing rules for the model, one fixed plain-language reason and one source. **The model never writes a research claim or a citation**; the person sees only these fixed sentences.
- **Gemini Decision** now also answers “Which kind of activity is this goal mostly about?” inside the existing first request (no extra call). A missing or unexpected answer falls back to the general rules.
- **Gemma** writes the three sizes under that kind's rules, plus: say exactly what and how much or where.
- The result carries `basis { domain, why, source }`. The goal editor shows “Why these steps” with the reason, the source and “General research, not personal medical advice.”
- Sources, checked by web search on 14 November 2026:
  - Noetel et al., *BMJ* 2024, “Effect of exercise for depression: systematic review and network meta-analysis” (218 studies; walking or jogging, yoga and strength training reduced depression; the authors rate confidence as low for walking or jogging and very low for the others). Used for movement and outdoors.
  - Richards et al., *The Lancet* 2016, COBRA trial (behavioural activation non-inferior to CBT at 12 months). Used for social and other.
  - The “say exactly what and how much” rule follows implementation-intention research (Gollwitzer and Sheeran 2006); a later, larger meta-analysis reports much smaller effects after bias correction, so it is used as a writing rule and is not cited to the person.
- Limits to state plainly: these studies support the general approach (exercise, behavioural activation). They do not show that these particular steps, or this app, help anyone. Nothing here is clinical validation.

Live results after the change (synthetic goals, whole pipeline 2.3–2.6 s each): Get outside more → outdoors → “Take ten slow breaths on the porch or balcony.” / “Walk to the nearest street corner and back.” / “Walk for 30 minutes through a local park.” Go to the gym more → movement → “Do 10 squats beside the bed” / “Walk for 10 minutes” / “Exercise at the gym for 30 minutes”. Call my friends more often → social → “Send one short text to one friend.” / “Leave one 2-minute voice message for one friend.” / “Call one friend for 30 minutes.” Vague goals (“Read more”, “a hobby I used to love”) are still judged too vague and give generic ladders.

## Demo morning

- When the hold completes, one soft wave of warm light spreads from the sun (not under Reduce Motion).
- The step card rises into place and now opens with what the alarm noticed and the size it led to, for example “A hard morning: 2 snoozes, 14 min to get up.” with Tiny / Small / Full and the chosen one filled. In the guided flow this replaces the small “Sensed · …” caption.
- Start demo now removes goals that were started but never approved, so it always opens on an empty goal field.

## Local browser preview

In demo mode only, the API now answers browser requests from `http://localhost` or `http://127.0.0.1` (any port) so the Expo web preview can use it. Requests still need the bearer token; other origins get no such headers (a preflight from another site returned 404).

## Evidence

- 366 API/shared tests, API and mobile typechecks and mobile lint pass. The local API was restarted and `/health` returned 200 locally and through the tunnel.
- Browser preview with the real models: Start demo → “Get outside more” from the ideas list → placeholder ladder → steps → “Why these steps” with the BMJ source → “How the AI checked these steps” showing Tiny 78% under 2 minutes, Small 82% 2 to 10 minutes, Full 49% 10 to 30 minutes → Approve → easier morning with the light wave and “An easier morning: up in 1 min, no snoozes.” with Full chosen → Next morning → 7:10 after two snoozes with “Your partner is already up.” → “A hard morning: 2 snoozes, 14 min to get up.” with Tiny chosen.
